Privacy Notice

Last updated: November 22, 2026

This notice explains how CHENGMU INDUSTRIAL CO., LTD. ("ChengMu") collects, uses and retains your data on this website, in accordance with Taiwan's Personal Data Protection Act. If this English version differs from the Traditional Chinese version, the Chinese version prevails.

What we collect

  • Online inquiry conversations: the questions you enter on the homepage and the replies you receive.
  • Connection information: your IP address and the approximate country and city derived from it.
  • Contact details you choose to provide: your name, phone, email or LINE ID, company or organisation, and the requirements you describe.
  • Meeting whiteboard passcode requests: your name, organisation, the email address that receives the passcode, and the meeting purpose if you choose to give one.

No login is required to use this website, and we never ask for your national ID number, date of birth or payment details.

How we use it

  • To answer your service inquiries and organise your requirements.
  • After you leave contact details, for our engineers to contact you, assess your needs and provide a quotation.
  • To issue online meeting whiteboard passcodes, email them to the address you provide, and limit repeated requests.
  • To prevent abuse (for example, a large number of messages in a short time) and keep the website running properly.

The data is used only for these purposes. It is never sold or provided to any other third party, except where required by law.

Processing and storage

Conversations are processed and stored by our cloud service providers on our behalf. After you leave contact details, a summary of your requirements is emailed to ChengMu's engineers.

When you request a meeting whiteboard passcode, your request details are sent to ChengMu's online meeting whiteboard system to issue the passcode, which is emailed to the address you provide, and ChengMu is notified. For how the whiteboard system handles request and meeting data, see the meeting whiteboard privacy notice.

Retention

General conversationsKept for 1 year, then deleted automatically.
Conversations and requests with contact detailsKept for 3 years, then deleted automatically.
Meeting whiteboard passcode requestsOnce a request is completed, this website keeps only a hash of the email address, the IP address, the conversation ID and the request time (to limit repeated requests), deleted automatically after 90 days; the passcode itself is never stored. The whiteboard system keeps request details for 90 days. The conversation during the request is kept like any general conversation.
Clients under contractRelated data is kept according to the contract and maintenance period.

You can also ask us to delete your data earlier at any time; see "Your rights" below.

Browser storage and analytics

  • The website keeps a random conversation ID in your browser to group messages from the same conversation; it is cleared when you close the tab. It also remembers your language choice (Chinese or English).
  • We use Cloudflare Web Analytics to count page views. It does not use cookies or track individuals.

  • request supplements or corrections;
  • request that we stop collecting, processing or using it;
  • request its deletion.
